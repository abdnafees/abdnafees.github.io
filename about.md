# About

I build things, take them apart, and write down what I learned.

I'm a software engineer in Lahore. Before that I spent my university years in makerspaces — running one, being a resident designer in another, and putting a lot of Raspberry Pis inside boxes I cut myself. Then I stopped for a while. Now I've started again, in a room in my house, with a pile of dead hardware and no deadline.

This site is where the writing goes. Embedded systems, kernels, machine learning small enough to run on a microcontroller, and the ordinary business of fixing things that don't work.

You can also find me as **brownnerd** — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@brownnerd), [X](https://twitter.com/_brownnerd), [Instagram](https://instagram.com/brwnnerd), [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/NmAVJGgw3F).

## A timeline

**2016**

- **Jul** — Joined Makeistan, Pakistan's first academic makerspace, at the Information Technology University of the Punjab. Started as a facilitator.
- **Oct** — Founded M-Lab, a makerspace at Forman Christian College.
- Through to 2018: a long run of IoT controllers, including *hakuna-matata* — home automation on a Raspberry Pi talking to a NodeMCU ESP8266.

**2017**

- **Mar** — RFID access controller on an ATmega AT89.
- **Jul–Sep** — Designer in Residence at Makeistan. Three months of building things that weren't quite engineering and weren't quite art.
- **The Water Saving Shower** — six weeks, one design partner, a shower head that uses less water per minute and nudges you toward a temperature shift once you've been in there too long. Market research done on Brandreth Road. Shown at TEDxITU.
- **A Light That Is Shy** — a lamp that would rather you didn't look at it.
- **How Will You Conduct Yourself In A Company Of Plants** — an installation where plants made sound when you interacted with them.
- **Nov** — Panoramic image generator in OpenCV, stitching with SIFT and SURF features.

**2018**

- **Oct 2017 – Jun 2018** — S.E.R.I.C., Smart Embedded Redundant IoT Camera. A facial recognition doorbell running entirely on-board, no cloud. My final year project.
- **Jun** — SERIC won Best of Punjab in Embedded Systems at COMPPEC'18, NUST College of EME. Also placed in the top hundred internationally at the Kazan OIC Entrepreneurship Boot-camp, and was pitched at the Telenor Velocity IoT Hackathon.
- Graduated with a BS in Computer Science from Forman Christian College.
- Tried to turn SERIC into a company. Got seriously ill instead.

**2019–2020**

- **Mar 2019 – Nov 2020** — Ran **Maker Baithak**, a gathering for people who make things. Invited guests, an audience that kept coming back, and a lot of conversations that had nothing to do with my day job.

**2023**

- Started a YouTube channel explaining computer science and programming in Urdu. Made a few videos. Stopped.

**2026**

- Started again. Built a lab at home — a rack of old ThinkPads, a NAS full of firmware, and a queue of broken devices. Working through kernel programming, embedded systems, and on-device machine learning, in that order.
- Writing it all down here.
